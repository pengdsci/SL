@ West Chester University (2019-present)

Programs Developed

  1. Data Science Minor Program (2025)
    • Approved by both the CS and Math departments, this joint program is currently progressing through the CAPC approval pipeline. The curriculum, co-developed by Dr. Amiruzzaman and me, is proposed for launch in Fall 2027.
  2. Graduate Data Science Concentration in Data Science (2019)
    • I created this program in fall 2019 upon joining WCU. Designed to equip students with advanced training in data science methodologies and applications, it prepares them for careers in modern statistical and data analytics fields. The concentration launched in fall 2020.
Graduate Courses Developed (2019)
    I built this four-course sequence for the graduate Data Science concentration to cover modern computational methods alongside cutting-edge techniques. STA551 - 553 deliver a project-based core, with STA503 offered as a programming prerequisite for incoming students.
  1. STA 503: Introduction to R
  2. STA 551: Foundations of Data Science
  3. STA 552: Applied Statistical Machine Learning
  4. STA 553: Data Visualization and Infographics
New Undergraduate Courses (2025)
    I developed these two new courses for the undergraduate Data Science minor to give students a solid grounding in both statistical methods and effective communication.
  1. STA 308: Statistics for Data Science
  2. STA 318: Data Visualization
Revised Graduate and Undergraduate Courses
  1. STA 490: Statistics Capstone (2025)
    • After six consecutive years of teaching and multiple revisions, this capstone course has been redesigned to help students complete end-to-end projects and communicate clearly with non-technical audiences. I submitted a proposal to make it a speaking- and writing-emphasis course. The revised capstone course has been approved by CAPC and will launch in Spring 2027.
  2. Online Graduate Courses in Data Science (2026)

    • STA 551: Introduction to Data Science
