Summary of Documented Professional Services

◇ Curriculum Development ◇ Services to the Research Community ◇ Consulting Services ◇ Institutional Services ◇

My professional services can be classified into the following four major categories: curriculum development, service to the research community, consulting services, and institutional services.

📚Curriculum Development↗️

Having trained in curriculum development and instructional design as an undergraduate and having applied these skills throughout my teaching career, I have been involved in creating new programs and courses in mathematics education, statistics, and data science at both the undergraduate and graduate levels across multiple institutions.

Since joining WCU in 2019, I designed the curriculum for the new graduate data science concentration within the Statistics MS program and co-designed the undergraduate data science minor with Computer Science. I have created six new courses for these new programs, revised three graduate courses for online delivery, and converted the undergraduate capstone (STA490) into speaking- and writing-emphasis courses.

Given the rapid evolution of data science and the difficulty of finding textbooks that cover the most current developments in the field, I continually update my course materials -including lecture notes, assignments, and assessments - each semester to reflect emerging trends and technologies.

I have taught introductory statistics for many years and have always tried to find ways to engage students. One challenge is that students often have limited support when practicing outside class. To address this, I have built and hosted 17 interactive statistics apps on a public server since 2022. These apps cover the main topics in introductory statistics and let students check their work independently. They are helpful not only for WCU students but also for learners beyond the university.

✍️ Service to the Research Community↗️

My professional service is most clearly exemplified by my long-standing role as an invited reviewer for Mathematical Reviews (MR), a premier publication of the American Mathematical Society—a position I have held continuously since 2012 (see invitation letter 🔗). In addition, I regularly serve as an ad hoc referee for a range of peer-reviewed journals and participate in various other service activities within the academic community.

In my work for Mathematical Reviews of American Mathematical Society, I critically evaluate and synthesize research spanning theoretical and methodological statistics, data science, and machine learning. I distill complex contributions into concise, accessible reviews that help researchers navigate an ever-expanding body of literature. As a journal referee, I assess each manuscript for rigor, originality, and significance, offering constructive feedback that enhances the clarity and impact of published work while supporting journals in upholding high editorial standards.

Taken together, these activities advance the production, dissemination, and integrity of scientific knowledge. Though often time-consuming and behind the scenes, I view them as an essential professional obligation. By supporting authors, editors, and fellow researchers, my service helps sustain a robust and trustworthy scholarly ecosystem. For these reasons, I consider reviewing and refereeing to be the most meaningful dimension of my professional service to AMS and beyond.


📊 Consulting Services↗️

My statistical consulting practice is grounded in a commitment to translating complex methodological principles into evidence-based solutions. Over the years, I have worked at the intersection of statistics and data science, bridging rigorous theory with practical problem-solving across a wide range of disciplines, including public health, medical research, social work, education, and business.

My consulting services are organized into two primary types:

Ad Hoc Consulting – This involves high-level consultations with practitioners, community organizations, and academic researchers. The emphasis is on providing methodological guidance - advising on study design and recommending appropriate analytical frameworks - without undertaking full data analysis or producing formal reports.

Formal Consulting – This encompasses deeper, sustained collaborations that result in concrete, written deliverables, such as comprehensive reports or analytic products. (See the list of consulting projects I have been involved in and for which I produced reports.)

Together, these two approaches translate statistical theory into tangible impact. By supporting evidence-based decision-making in real-world settings, my consulting practice not only complements my scholarly work but also actively contributes to the advancement of knowledge and practice across multiple fields.


📜 Institutional Services ↗️

I have a long and extensive history of institutional service, spanning over two decades across two universities. My service is characterized by deep involvement at the departmental, college, and university levels. Since joining the WCU mathematics faculty, I have consistently participated in a wide variety of committees and have provided quality service, particularly through the application of my disciplinary expertise.
