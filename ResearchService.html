Service to the Research Community

I have been continuously devoting a significant amount of time to serving the broader mathematical and statistical research community in two major ways: writing reviews for the American Mathematical Society's official journal Mathematical Reviews as an invited reviewer since 2012, and serving as an ad hoc referee for numerous peer-reviewed journals in statistics and data science.
